What it takes off your plate

The routine questions that eat your day, handled the moment they arrive.

Answers from your own list

Prices, timings, address, payment options. It only uses the information you give us, and says so when it doesn’t know.

“Is a checkup painful?”
“No, it’s painless and takes about 20 minutes.”

Books the appointment

Asks for the name, service and time, checks what is free, and confirms. A slot can’t be booked twice.

“Book me Saturday 3 pm.”
“That slot is free. Shall I confirm?”

Hands over to you

Refunds, complaints or a customer who wants a person: the assistant steps back and you take over the chat.

“I’ve informed our team, they will reply shortly.”

Questions owners ask

What if it says something wrong?

It answers only from the price list and FAQs you give us. If the answer isn’t there, or the customer is upset, it tells them your team will reply and alerts you instead of guessing.

Can my customers still talk to a person?

Yes. Anyone can ask for a person at any time. While you handle a chat, the assistant stays silent so you never talk over each other.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

The assistant runs on a number registered with WhatsApp’s business platform. We’ll check your current number and tell you the simplest option before you pay anything.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Hindi and Hinglish. It replies in the language the customer uses.

What does WhatsApp itself charge?

WhatsApp bills per message for some message types, and the rates change from time to time. We pass these through at cost and give you an estimate based on your expected chats.

What happens to my customers’ chats?

Chats are stored only to run the service and produce your reports. Read the privacy page for the details, and ask us to delete your data at any time.
